TASK 9 EVIDENCE: PUBLISH ACCOUNT-LEVEL MEMBERSHIP AND REGISTRY STATE ATOMICALLY
================================================================================

1. Architectural Overview & Split-Brain Prevention
--------------------------------------------------
Previously, account pool membership and model registry state lived in separate structures:
- Active account pool in `AppState.pool: ArcSwap<PoolSnapshot>`
- Catalog state in `CatalogManager.active_snapshot: ArcSwap<RegistrySnapshot>`

This posed a split-brain risk where concurrent readers could observe:
- A new account pool generation paired with a stale registry snapshot, or
- A refreshed registry snapshot paired with a stale account pool generation, or
- Models advertised without routable accounts (or vice versa).

Resolution implemented in Task 9:
- Introduced `RuntimeComposition` (type-aliased with `PoolSnapshot` for complete backward compatibility):
  ```rust
  pub struct RuntimeComposition {
      pub generation: u64,
      pub members: Vec<Arc<AccountMember>>,
      pub models: Vec<ModelEntry>,
      pub registry: Arc<RegistrySnapshot>,
  }
  pub type PoolSnapshot = RuntimeComposition;
  ```
- Introduced `UnifiedRuntimeState` (aka `RuntimeState`):
  - Encapsulates `pool: Arc<ArcSwap<RuntimeComposition>>`
  - Encapsulates `generation_seq: AtomicU64`
  - Encapsulates `RefreshCoordinator` for single-flight serialization and coalescing
- Candidate Composition Construction (`compute_candidate_composition`):
  - Validates candidate `RegistrySnapshot` invariants (`registry.validate()`).
  - If invalid (e.g. alias cycles, duplicate bindings), candidate is rejected and NOT published.
  - Recomputes candidate effective models by merging provider kinds, generic account declarations, and registered bindings.
  - Ensures candidate routable accounts are fully queryable (`routable_accounts_for_model`).
- Atomic Publication:
  - Both accounts and registry advance together in a single `ArcSwap::store` call.
  - Readers on the relay hot path perform lock-free `pool.load()` or `runtime.load()`.
  - Zero locks held on the read path. Zero locks held during remote HTTP fetching.
  - Zero possibility of mixed-generation observations.

2. Concurrency & Coalescing Coordinator
---------------------------------------
- `RefreshCoordinator` serializes candidate composition building while coalescing overlapping triggers.
- 100 concurrent triggers produce monotonic unique published generations without deadlock.
- Invalid candidates fail safely without updating the pointer or advancing generation counter.
